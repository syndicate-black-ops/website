A `xen_plantlight` is a Xen plant with a glowing light that pulls back into the ground when a player comes within
about 80 units of it. Its **Target** gets On when the light comes out and Off when it pulls back.

## See also

- [xen_plantlight on TWHL](https://twhl.info/wiki/page/xen_plantlight) - the Half-Life entity

## Changes since 1.38

- Fixed a removed `xen_plantlight` leaving its glow in the air, and a removed `xen_tree` leaving its attack area
  behind. The game removed the entity but not its parts. This was a bug in SBO 1.38
