A `weapon_warhammer` gives a player who walks onto it the Fusion Warhammer, a melee weapon that releases a plasma
blast when it hits. It fires its **Target** when a player takes it, and it doesn't come back after that. Its world
model is the crowbar.

## Notes

- Only living players pick it up
- A player who already has it leaves it on the floor
- It falls to the floor when the map starts and lies flat there
