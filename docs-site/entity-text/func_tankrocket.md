A `func_tankrocket` is a mounted rocket launcher. It aims at targets by itself, or a player controls it from a
[`func_tankcontrols`](func_tankcontrols.md), and it has the keys of a [`func_tank`](func_tank.md).

## Notes

- The player at the controls gets the kills of the gun: the frags, the Co-op XP and the line in the kill feed. While
  nobody is at the controls, no player gets them

## See also

- [`func_tank`](func_tank.md) - a mounted gun
- [`func_tankcontrols`](func_tankcontrols.md) - the place where a player takes the controls
- [func_tankrocket on TWHL](https://twhl.info/wiki/page/func_tankrocket) - the Half-Life entity

## Changes since 1.38

- You can use the new keys and flags of [`func_tank`](func_tank.md#changes-since-138)
- The player at the controls gets the kills. In SBO 1.38, as in Half-Life, the kills went to the gun and the
  player got nothing
