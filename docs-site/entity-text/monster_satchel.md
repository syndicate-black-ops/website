A `monster_satchel` is a live satchel charge lying in the map, and its explosion deals a random 200 to 300 damage.

## Notes

- Triggering the charge by its **Name** makes it explode
- A charge that a door runs into explodes

## See also

- [`monster_tripmine`](monster_tripmine.md) - a live trip mine
- [monster_satchel on TWHL](https://twhl.info/wiki/page/monster_satchel) - the Half-Life entity

## Changes since 1.38

- A satchel charge deals 200 to 300 damage, as its original description says. In SBO 1.38 it always dealt 200
- Fixed a satchel charge or a trip mine under a door jamming the door. The door now sets it off. This was a bug in SBO
