The Scientist is a passive human with 100 health who runs from danger and follows a player who presses the use key on
it.

## Attributes

- **Body** (`body`) - Sets the Scientist's head, which also sets the pitch of its voice, and Luther also has darker
  hands
    - Random (-1, default) - Each Scientist picks one of the four heads when it spawns
    - If you leave this key empty, it has the head Glasses

## Notes

- Players earn XP for the damage they deal to it. See [Co-op](../../game-modes/coop.md#xp) for more details
- Its **Followage policy** can change whether it follows a player. See [Common keys](common-keys.md#talkmonster) for
  more details
