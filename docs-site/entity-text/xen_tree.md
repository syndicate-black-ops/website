A `xen_tree` strikes with its branch when a player or monster comes close in front of it, or when something hurts it,
and it deals 25 damage to each player and monster in front of it.

## See also

- [xen_tree on TWHL](https://twhl.info/wiki/page/xen_tree) - the Half-Life entity

## Changes since 1.38

- Fixed a removed `xen_plantlight` leaving its glow in the air, and a removed `xen_tree` leaving its attack area
  behind. The game removed the entity but not its parts. This was a bug in SBO 1.38
