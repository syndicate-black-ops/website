A `func_tankcontrols` marks the place where a player takes the controls of a [`func_tank`](func_tank.md) with their
use key. The gun must have the flag **Controllable**, and the player then aims it where they look and fires it with
their attack key.

## Attributes

- **Tank entity name** (`target`) - Names the gun that a player standing in this brush takes over, and with several
  guns of that name, the first one
