An `item_battery` is a battery for the HEV suit. A player with less than 100 armor picks it up, but it adds no armor,
because a battery gives 0 armor in SBO. Like every item, it doesn't come back after a player picks it up, and players
get armor from a [`func_recharge`](func_recharge.md).
