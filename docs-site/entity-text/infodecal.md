An `infodecal` puts a decal on the surface it touches, within 5 units. Without a name, the decal appears when the map
starts, and with a name, it is sprayed once when you trigger it, so players who join later don't see it.

## Attributes

- **Name** (`targetname`) - Triggering the entity sprays the decal. See [Common keys](common-keys.md#targetname)
- **texture** (`texture`) - Sets the decal from `decals.wad` that is put on the surface
