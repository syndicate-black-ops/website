A `weapon_sw` gives a player who walks onto it the Vindico Plasma Sniper Rifle, which fires plasma shots that are
faster when the player zooms in. It fires its **Target** when a player takes it, and it doesn't come back after that.

## Notes

- It comes with 5 rounds
- Only living players pick it up
- A player who already has this weapon takes only its ammo. A player who also has full ammo leaves it on the floor
- It falls to the floor when the map starts and lies flat there
