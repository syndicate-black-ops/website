A `func_traincontrols` marks the place on a [`func_tracktrain`](func_tracktrain.md) where a player drives it. A player
inside this space on the train drives it with their move keys, and the brush itself is removed when the map starts.

## Attributes

- **Train Name** (`target`) - Names the `func_tracktrain` that these controls drive
